Ghono Kalo chul tar
Horiner moto ankhi,
Sei ankhitei mon ta amar
Guchiye jeno rakhi.


Tar Kesher gondho tane more
tar thot duti lobh dekhaye,
Takiye theke jug kete jaye
Tobu Chuute je pari na haye


Tar konthe jeno dhala modhu
Sunle ami bhabi sudhu,
E kon devi ti moirte neme
Holo je amar bodhu?


Ajo ami ekla bose rater bela bhabi
Se na hole jibon amar hoto je kebol phnaki,
Tar bepare sob bollam, bollam na se ke,
Ar keu noye se, se je amar, sudhu amar rosogolla je se.
